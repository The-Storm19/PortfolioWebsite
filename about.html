Artist Bio

Analiese Schreier

Analiese Schreier is a Junior at Houghton University working toward a BFA in Applied Design and Visual Communications and a Minor in Art Business. This combines her creativity and love of art while applying it to the practical world around her.

Her branding and logo design work has been featured in books published by Masthof Publishing, as well as the logo and digital presence of a private LLC company. These showcase her ability to create cohesive and professional visual deliverables across print and digital platforms.

She is based in Chester County, Pennsylvania, and has interned in both the publishing world as well as several projects in company branding. She continues to develop her love of photography and photo editing and incorporates them into her graphic designs.
