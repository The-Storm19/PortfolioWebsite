About

A studio that treats print and photography as one practice

Analiese Schreier trained as a graphic designer and picked up a camera to understand her own briefs better. The two practices are hard to separate — most identity projects now include a photographic commission, and most photographic series end up typeset and bound.

The studio works mostly with cultural institutions, independent publishers, and small record labels — clients who print things and want those things to last longer than a scroll. Projects are taken on slowly, each seen through from first sketch to final proof.

Based in Houghton, NY.
